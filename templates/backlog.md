# backlog.md schema

Open entries: 30 words, 1 sentence, 1 bullet. Closed entries: 10 words, 1 sentence, 1 bullet.

A living doc. Rewrite it in place whenever it stops matching the product.

Each entry records the reported problem or completed outcome. Every entry keeps one ID for life: `B-<N>` for a bug, `I-<N>` for an improvement, `F-<N>` for a feature phase.

Open entries sit in priority order. The top entry goes first. Polish work records its detail and recovery state in `polish-plan.md`.

Rewrite completed entries under Closed and keep them there.

## Bugs

- `B-<N>` <what's wrong, where it shows up, and what it breaks>

### Closed

- `<ID>` <what it was, compressed>

## Improvements

- `I-<N>` <what would get better, and why it's worth doing>

### Closed

- `<ID>` <what it was, compressed>

## Feature phases

Big enough to earn its own future roadmap phase, not a quick fold-in.

- `F-<N>` <the feature, and the value it unlocks>

### Closed

- `<ID>` <what it was, compressed>
